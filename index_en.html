❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does the app require an internet connection?
    This is a fully offline utility app. All features work without an internet connection.
  • Where is my data stored?
    All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • Can I recover accidentally deleted data?
    Since data is stored locally on your device only, deleted data cannot be recovered. Please use caution when deleting.
